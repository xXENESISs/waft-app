## Important game documentation discrepancy

Matamata's official `Ancestral Retreat` text says the **next** direct damage instance is reduced. `applyAncestralRetreatDefense` does not consume its active flag when it processes an impact. A deterministic regression check observes eight reductions during an octopus Tentacle Storm in the same turn. This is described in the dossier, not repaired in the combat engine. Any gameplay decision belongs to a separately authorized task.

The sloth selector's shorthand that lichens "double" the other colony is explained precisely in the editorial notes: extra random attempts for algae/fungi, accelerated bacterial progress, and increased mite discount. It does not imply guaranteed doubled healing or a blanket damage multiplier.

## Architecture

- `js/codex-data.js`: retained original catalogue and legacy provenance; no numerical combat copies.
- `js/codex-model.js`: archive adapter; reads canonical game definitions and keeps Spanish/English names searchable.
- `js/codex-research.js`: field-level biology, geographic summaries, martial history, reference registry and fiction connections.
- `js/codex-tactics.js`: qualitative tactics and detailed notes, reviewed against the source fingerprints.
- `js/codex-ui.js`, `css/codex.css`: shared rendering, responsive UI, filters, art dialog, form explorer and accessible numerical views.
- `docs/codex/reviewed-game-sources.json`: hashes of the definitions, engine and effects used for editorial review. If these change, CI requires review of the explanatory text; never simply refresh fingerprints to silence a failure.
- `scripts/build-codex-images.py`: derivative encoding only; never crops or replaces original artwork. Requires Pillow, not a runtime browser dependency.
- `scripts/build-codex-review.mjs [output]`: packages a clearly labeled, static review snapshot. No server or hosting action.

## Editorial coverage and limitations

Four model dossiers document both biology and martial history: Matamata/BJJ, Coconut Octopus/Arnis, Fennec/Tahtib, Peregrine Falcon/Taekkyeon. They still mark individual unavailable fields (e.g. lifespan) as pending.

Darwin's frog, pale-throated three-toed sloth and emerald jewel wasp have additional sourced biological sections. Frog and sloth do not acquire an invented martial discipline. Wasp's Dim Mak label remains artistic; its history is not represented as verified. Six profiles have referenced regional distribution summaries; the wasp does not yet have one.

The remaining 29 dossiers retain illustrations, inherited identifications, official game data where available and explicit research status. Their biology and martial history are not claimed complete. Longevity, current conservation assessments, local range distinctions and specialist cultural traditions require further work.

Geographic alternatives organize sourced regions and link to published distribution material (including BirdLife's map). No arbitrary map, approximate polygon or national outline is presented as species presence. Introduced/historical areas are separately described or explicitly unreviewed.

References were reviewed using institutional/scientific search results and retrieved pages. Some institutional sites challenge automated page retrieval; a working URL in the registry is not a claim that every source was exhaustively reloaded or every linked map tested on Android. Historical turtle references are dated and explicitly not reused as current taxonomy/CITES assessments.

Fennec is a useful source-quality example: Smithsonian mentions Arabia, while the specialist canid account ends at Sinai and San Diego Zoo notes uncertainty for Arabia. The dossier follows the specialist range and states the uncertainty.

## Verification performed

- `node scripts/validate-codex.mjs`: six collections, 36 distinct records, exact 26-game-ID coverage, original/derivative artwork, legacy migrations, references, source fingerprints and accessible page fallbacks.
- `node --test tests/codex.test.mjs`: eight tests passed, including all attributes/forms, concept separation, accent/multi-term search, geographic provenance, octopus charge preservation, actual larval allocation and the Matamata discrepancy.
- Browser: all 36 shared dossiers rendered; 26 had eight official base attributes and 10 had none. All six collection lists had expected counts (6/5/6/7/6/6). No missing in-page anchors or horizontal page overflow found.
- Responsive browser frames: all 36 dossiers at 390 px; home, longest collection and complex/long-name profiles at 320, 360 and 768 px. No page overflow. These are CSS viewport checks in Chromium, not physical Android emulation.
- Interaction: accented search, result counts, archive-only filter, region empty state, reset, return from dossier preserving query, legacy typo URL, missing-ID recovery, octopus form switching and art-dialog open/close.
- Application errors: none observed in the tested paths; browser extension metadata errors were excluded as unrelated.
- `git diff --check` clean. Protected game files unchanged relative to the starting commit.

Remaining verification: physical Android browser behavior, assistive-technology testing with a screen reader, and real network/device performance. No automated balance experiments, statistical matchup claims, Academy execution or production smoke test were performed.
