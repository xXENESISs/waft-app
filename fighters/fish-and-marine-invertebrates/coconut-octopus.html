Coconut Octopus

Amphioctopus marginatus
RegionIndo-West Pacific
HabitatCoastal muddy and sandy seafloors
DisciplineArnis / Eskrima (Kali)

The Coconut Octopus (Amphioctopus marginatus) is a small Indo-West Pacific octopus known for using portable shelters. It lives on sandy and muddy seafloors, where it can collect clam shells, coconut halves and even discarded objects, carry them while moving, and assemble them into a protective refuge when needed. This unusual behaviour is one of the clearest examples of tool use documented in an invertebrate.

Core mechanics
Passive ability

Cephalopod Adaptation

8 adaptation charges per combat. Before acting, the octopus can switch between Base, Offensive, Defensive and Evasive Form without spending the turn. The first transformation is free; leaving a form clears that form’s accumulated passive stacks.

Special attack · Offensive · 4 hits

Perfect Adaptation

While in Base Form, choose a balanced version of Tentacle Storm, Coconut Fortress or Ink Sea. It does not change form and does not consume an adaptation charge.
